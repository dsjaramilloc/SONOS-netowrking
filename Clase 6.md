## WLAN Threats

- <DOS (DENIAL OF SERVICES)>: Overwhelms the WLAN with traffic, preventing legitimate users from accessing the network.


- <ROGUE ACCESS POINTS (UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS POINTS):> An unauthorized or fake access point that mimics a legitimate one, tricking users into connecting and exposing their data.

-<MAN IN THE MIDDLE (MITM):> An attacker intercepts communication between a user and the network, allowing data to be monitored or altered without detection
